Guidance and settings
Workout library
Runs
Threshold blocks
2 km easy warm-up, controlled threshold reps, easy cool-down. Respect the pace. Not a time trial.
Speed play
400 m to 1000 m repeats around 10 km pace. Smooth, fast, repeatable. Stop chasing pace if form drops.
Strength running
Uphill treadmill/run intervals at 7/10. Useful close to race day, but not every week.
Long easy
60–75 min easy. This replaces JK’s bigger 10–16 km prescriptions when needed.
Recovery run
25–45 min very easy. Builds volume without adding stress.
JK-style finishers
Engine EMOM
20–24 min. Row, ski, bike and wall balls. Hold 7/10 and repeat clean outputs.
Overhead support
Push press and devil press support wall balls and burpee tolerance. Keep the dose small.
Sled / erg
Sled push or pull paired with bike, ski or row. Technique and repeatability over max effort.
Wall-ball / row-ski
Ladders or EMOMs to build wall-ball rhythm under breathing fatigue.
Lunge / carry
Sandbag lunges, farmer carries and sled pulls. Brace, breathe and keep steps controlled.
Burpee rhythm
Burpees or BBJ mixed with ski, row or run. Smooth floor breathing. No panic reps.
Friday rule
Friday pairs F45 or easy aerobic with one 15–20 min complementary finisher. Event and taper Fridays switch to recovery or a short primer.

Training principles

Intention: get ready for Melbourne solo without copying a two-hour-a-day program you cannot recover from.

  • Dec 12 is the A-race. Everything points there.
  • Oct 25 Half Rox is a simulation. Race it, learn from it, then keep building.
  • Use JK’s structure at 70%. Keep the best sessions and cut duplicate volume.
  • Train 60–90 minutes on 5–6 days. Friday is the controlled sixth day.
  • Three stress points. Quality run, Thursday F45 Hyrox class and long easy run.
  • Easy volume stays easy. Build the engine and protect the calf.
  • Final 10-day taper. One last simulation, then freshen up.
