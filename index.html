15–25-min Finishers
JK Engine EMOM
20 min EMOM. 5 rounds of 4 mins. Min 1: 15/12 cal row. Min 2: 15/12 cal ski. Min 3: 15 wall balls at race weight, add 1 rep only if clean. Min 4: 15/12 cal bike. Hold 7/10.
JK Active Recovery AMRAP
20–25 min AMRAP. 500m ski, 25 air squats, 15 push-ups, 200m easy run. Hold 6/10. Smooth volume, not a smash.
Wall-ball Progression
15–20 min EMOM. 10–15 wall balls every minute. Add reps only when breathing and squat depth stay clean. Stop before ugly reps.
DB Overhead Block
16–20 min EMOM. Odd mins: 12–15 DB push press. Even mins: 4–6 devil press. This targets your race limiter. Legs drive every rep.
Ski / Burpee Rhythm
15–20 min AMRAP. 250m ski + 8–10 burpees or BBJ. Keep breathing steady. No frantic reps.
Sled Push / Pull
20 min alternating EMOM. Odd mins: 12.5m sled push. Even mins: 12.5m sled pull. Technique first. Full control.
Lunge / Carry Block
20 min EMOM. Odd mins: 20m walking lunges. Even mins: 40m farmers carry. Brace, breathe, move without rushing.
Mixed Station Coverage
24 min EMOM. Rotate 4 stations for 6 rounds: row or ski, wall balls, lunges or carry, sled or burpees. Use this when a station has been missed recently.

Training Principles

Intention: build a strong Hyrox engine and better station endurance without turning every day into a race.

  • Train 60–90 minutes most days. Enough work to improve. Not so much you break.
  • Three stress points. Tuesday quality run. Thursday F45 Hyrox class. Weekend long easy run.
  • Easy volume matters. Recovery runs, long easy runs and mellow surf build the engine.
  • Weights stay in. They keep muscle, strength and joint resilience.
  • Finishers are 15–25 minutes. They turn gym strength into repeatable Hyrox station work.
  • Use JK’s structure. EMOMs, AMRAPs, row, ski, wall balls, bike and steady 6–7/10 pacing.
  • Adapt where needed. Sleds, carries, lunges, DB push press and devil press cover your Hyrox gaps.
  • VO2 is every 4 weeks. Useful, but costly.
  • Weeks 4 and 8 benchmark. Use the Thursday Hyrox class as the simulation focus. Do not add extra.
