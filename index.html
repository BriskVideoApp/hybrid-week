Workout Library
13 weeks from Sept 12: JK structure at about 70%, capped at 60–90 minutes on 5–6 days each week.
Runs
Threshold blocks
2 km easy warm-up, controlled threshold reps, easy cool-down. Respect the pace. Not a time trial.
Speed play
400 m to 1000 m repeats around 10 km pace. Smooth, fast, repeatable. Stop chasing pace if form drops.
Strength running
Uphill treadmill/run intervals at 7/10. Useful close to race day, but not every week.
Long easy
60–75 min easy. This replaces JK’s bigger 10–16 km prescriptions when needed.
Recovery run
25–45 min very easy. Builds volume without adding stress.
JK-style finishers
Engine EMOM
20–24 min. Row, ski, bike and wall balls. Hold 7/10 and repeat clean outputs.
Overhead support
Small dose only. Push press and devil press support wall balls and burpee tolerance, but true HYROX stations stay the priority.
Sled / erg
Sled push or pull paired with bike/ski/row. Technique and repeatability over max effort.
Wall-ball / row-ski
Ladders or EMOMs to build wall-ball rhythm under breathing fatigue.
Lunge / carry
Sandbag lunges, farmer carries, sled pulls. Brace, breathe, keep steps controlled.
Burpee rhythm
Burpees or BBJ mixed with ski/row/run. Smooth floor breathing. No panic reps.
Friday rule
Complement, do not compete
Friday pairs F45 or easy aerobic with one 15–20 min finisher that fills a station gap left by Monday and Wednesday. After a hard Thursday, keep it controlled so Saturday’s long run stays easy and useful.
Event and taper weeks
The Friday slot becomes recovery aerobic after Half Rox, a short recovery touch eight days out, and an optional 8–12 min primer on race eve.

Training Principles

Intention: get ready for Melbourne solo without copying a 2-hour-a-day program you cannot recover from.

  • Dec 12 is the A-race. Everything points there.
  • Oct 25 Half Rox is a simulation. Race it, learn from it, then keep building.
  • Use JK’s structure at 70%. Keep the best sessions. Cut duplicate volume.
  • Train 60–90 minutes on 5–6 days. Friday is the controlled sixth day: F45 or easy aerobic plus one complementary finisher.
  • Three stress points. Quality run, Thursday F45 Hyrox class, long easy run.
  • Easy volume stays easy. This builds your engine and protects your calf.
  • Finishers are specific. Row, ski, wall balls, sleds, lunges, carries, burpees, DB overhead.
  • Deload before taper. Reduce fatigue before trying to sharpen.
  • Final 10 days taper. One last simulation, then freshen up.
